# Mood: Calm Live Tutor Cards

## Essence
The lesson should feel like a quiet one-to-one voice call whose transcript settles into place one sentence at a time. The interface stays pale, spacious, and almost still; only the current voice card and its green signal carry energy.

## Colour
- **Temperature:** Neutral-cool white with a soft violet teaching layer.
- **Palette:** `#FFFFFF` paper white, `#F5F3FF` Lindsey card, `#3A334B` learner card, `#7BDC42` live voice green, `#6B55DC` lesson violet, `#827B8C` translation grey.
- **Contrast:** Medium; dark text on pale cards, with green reserved for live audio.
- **Saturation:** Muted except for the voice signal.

## Typography
- **Character:** Conversational and readable, without dashboard heaviness.
- **Weight distribution:** 400-500 for spoken English and translations; 600-650 only for headings, speaker labels, and keywords.
- **Density:** Airy. One sentence per card with short line lengths.
- **Suggested pairings:** System sans-serif for both English and Chinese; no decorative display face.

## Space & Layout
- **Rhythm:** A vertical sequence of independent sentence cards separated by generous breathing room.
- **Grid character:** Stable main conversation column with an optional lesson-material panel.
- **Scale relationships:** Conversation text leads; speaker labels and translations remain subordinate.

## Texture & Material
- **Surface quality:** Matte, flat, softly rounded.
- **Material references:** Light paper cards rather than glass panels.
- **Finish:** Minimal shadow; state changes are expressed through clarity and motion.

## Emotional Register
The learner should feel that Lindsey is present and speaking now, without being overwhelmed by a wall of transcript. Completed sentences feel dependable and easy to revisit; the active sentence feels alive but temporary.

## Design Principles
- **One sentence, one card** — never place an entire turn in one dense block.
- **One source of speech text** — the conversation card is both subtitle and history.
- **Voice owns the green** — use bright green only for the three-arc audio signal.
- **Clarity means completion** — the active sentence is softly blurred; a completed sentence becomes crisp.
- **Material stays separate** — model answers belong in the lesson-material panel, not duplicated in chat.
- **Portrait panel gives direction** — the area below Lindsey shows the current learning phase and one short task cue, never a second transcript.

## References
- `codex-clipboard-7add8323-705b-44ce-b960-9ad73de670c6.png`: contributes the single-sentence grey card, right-aligned three-arc green voice mark, blurred active card, and crisp completed cards.

## Anti-References
- Repeating the same speech under the portrait, in chat, and in a subtitle bar.
- Heavy bold text across whole paragraphs.
- Large glowing gradients, glass effects, or dashboard-like status panels.
- Revealing a complete sentence before its audio has played.

## Agent Instructions
Render every Lindsey sentence as a separate pale card. While its audio is playing, obscure the text enough that individual letters cannot be read and animate only the three green arcs at its right edge. When the sentence has been played, remove the blur, stop the breathing animation, and reveal its translation. Use the portrait-side card only for the current phase and a short task cue; never duplicate the transcript there.
